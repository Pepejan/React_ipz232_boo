## 3. Data Collection & Processing (GDPR Compliance)

### 3.1 Data We Collect

Emoji Match Game is a **client-side only** application. We do **NOT** operate any servers or backend services. All data is stored **locally on your device** in your browser's `localStorage`.

The following data is stored locally:

| Data Type | Purpose | Storage Location | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Game settings (pairs count, flip speed, theme) | Persist user preferences | `localStorage` | Until you clear browser data |
| User name | Personalize the profile page | `localStorage` | Until you clear browser data |
| Game history (moves, time, date) | Show statistics and history | `localStorage` | Until you clear browser data |
| Cookie consent preference | Remember your GDPR consent choice | `localStorage` | 365 days |

### 3.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

We do **NOT** collect, transmit, or store:
- Personal identification information (name, email, address)
- IP addresses
- Browser fingerprints or tracking cookies
- Analytics or telemetry data
- Payment information

### 3.3 Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

Since all data is stored only on your own device and never transmitted to any server, the Application does not engage in any data *processing* in the GDPR sense that would require a legal basis. Your data never leaves your browser.

### 3.4 Your Rights Under GDPR

As a user in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

- **Right of Access** – You can inspect all stored data by opening your browser's DevTools → Application → Local Storage.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")** – You can delete all data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or by using the browser settings.
- **Right to Portability** – All data is stored in plain JSON in localStorage and can be exported via browser DevTools.
- **Right to Rectification** – You can change your user name and game settings at any time within the Application.
- **Right to Object** – Since no data is transmitted to third parties, there is nothing to object to beyond clearing your local data.

### 3.5 Cookies

This Application uses **localStorage** (not cookies) for data persistence. A cookie consent banner is displayed to inform you of this usage and to obtain your consent where required by law (ePrivacy Directive / GDPR).

**Types of storage used:**
- `emoji-match-settings` – Functional (required for the game to remember your preferences)
- `emoji-match-user` – Functional (required to maintain your profile and history)
- `cookie-consent` – Functional (remembers your consent choice)

No third-party cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics libraries are used.
